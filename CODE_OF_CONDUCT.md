# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include being considerate, respectful, and collaborative; accepting constructive feedback; and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults or personal attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The repository owner is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that are not aligned with this Code. Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile: <https://github.com/BPHvZ>.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
