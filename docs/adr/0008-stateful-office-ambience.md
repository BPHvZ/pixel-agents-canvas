# ADR 0008: Stateful office ambience

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-17

## Context

The office should feel inhabited and expressive while remaining a truthful, read-only view of provider activity. The three examples use different boundaries: [Agent Virtual Office](https://github.com/KbWen/agent-virtual-office/blob/460d71db3b41746748e53e881d7ecd08e1207f60/src/systems/officeLife.js) stages coffee-break scenes and reduces ambient events during a live activity pulse; [Agent Office](https://github.com/Pixel-Process-UG/agent-office/blob/da62eee3bb054d0500b15ace53dd005adcfa512d/src/WelcomeOnboarding.tsx) uses user-configured focus/presence for agents it manages; [Pixel Agents](https://github.com/pixel-agents-hq/pixel-agents/blob/main/webview-ui/src/office/components/ToolOverlay.tsx) displays provider-driven tool/subagent activity in overlays. This dashboard adopts their separation of character animation, office context, and real activity, but not user-invented work states, orchestration, or raw tool-content display.

## Source patterns reviewed

- [Agent Virtual Office `App.jsx`](https://github.com/KbWen/agent-virtual-office/blob/460d71db3b41746748e53e881d7ecd08e1207f60/src/App.jsx) composes the pixel office with a control panel and activity feed. Its [`officeLife.js`](https://github.com/KbWen/agent-virtual-office/blob/460d71db3b41746748e53e881d7ecd08e1207f60/src/systems/officeLife.js) stages a tea/coffee break (one character drinks while others chat); real activity retains priority and ambient frequency drops when a live pulse is present.
- [Agent Office onboarding](https://github.com/Pixel-Process-UG/agent-office/blob/da62eee3bb054d0500b15ace53dd005adcfa512d/src/WelcomeOnboarding.tsx) and its [agent data model](https://github.com/Pixel-Process-UG/agent-office/blob/da62eee3bb054d0500b15ace53dd005adcfa512d/src/data.ts) show focus, presence, and critical-task values are user-configured for managed agents. [`world.ts`](https://github.com/Pixel-Process-UG/agent-office/blob/da62eee3bb054d0500b15ace53dd005adcfa512d/src/world.ts) maps idle, active, meeting, paused, and blocked states to sprites.
- [Pixel Agents `OfficeCanvas.tsx`](https://github.com/pixel-agents-hq/pixel-agents/blob/main/webview-ui/src/office/components/OfficeCanvas.tsx) renders agent state in the scene; [`ToolOverlay.tsx`](https://github.com/pixel-agents-hq/pixel-agents/blob/main/webview-ui/src/office/components/ToolOverlay.tsx) exposes subtask titles on hover and live tool activity on selection. This informs the legible overlay/feed pattern, not permission to display raw tool details here.

These are source-level implementation patterns, not proof that our providers expose the same events or that fictional scenes are real activity.

## Decision

- Keep a **factual layer** and a **fictional office layer** distinct. Provider identity, relationships, state, freshness, titles, and lifecycle remain authoritative and visibly available. Only observed provider transitions enter the global recent-activity feed; fictional scenes, quips, props, and setup actions never do.
- Use distinct, accessible status cues: focused desk behavior for working; a visible wait cue; attentive approval/blocked cue; neutral issue cue for failure; brief completion flourish followed by rest; neutral pose for unknown. Preserve readable state/freshness labels and reduced-motion support.
- Use authored, state-triggered decorative dialogue with an explicit persona-flavor label and cooldown. Stable voice flavors (curious, dry-witted, earnest, calm, upbeat) are decorative, not provider personality/role/capability. Playful lines may appear in any state but cannot mock or blame the user/task, and never obscure an actionable approval/error cue.
- Office vignettes are fictional and may involve characters across provider-reported clusters; they do not assert collaboration. Only characters with a current explicit provider `idle` state may participate. Idle characters may interact while other agents work; if a participant transitions to working, dismiss the whole vignette and return characters to their desks. Stale, unknown, and disconnected agents do not participate. Vignettes are frequent (about every 10–30 seconds while eligible agents are present) and use a shuffle cycle that avoids repeating a template until the bank is exhausted. Abandoned-cluster ambience uses the same cadence and non-repeating cycle.
- The authored idle-scene bank includes coffee/tea, stretching/walking, plant care, desk tidying, generic reading, light antics, and friendly group chat. Separate controls for office vignettes and dialogue are on by default; disabling either leaves factual status and feed intact.
- Use deterministic local scene motifs sourced only from the provider-supplied session title, never prompt/transcript/tool content. The catalog has work-type and technical-area axes (implementation, debugging, refactoring, review, testing, docs, design, research, planning, performance, accessibility, security, data/schema, APIs, dependencies, CI/build, release, migration, cleanup, configuration; frontend, backend, storage, APIs, mobile, desktop, cloud/infra, CLI/tooling, ML/AI, graphics/games). Show at most four high-confidence motifs per session cluster (up to two from each axis); use neutral decor when uncertain. Motifs decorate the session cluster, not agent identity. A provider title may contain sensitive text: show it in the roster by default with global and per-session hide controls. Hiding title text does not hide its derived motif; explain this in the control. When a session ends, remove its title and motif.
- Office props persist for the open canvas lifetime. Props are removed only through visible cleanup or when the office closes. Any currently idle agent may tidy a prop across clusters. A newly arriving agent has one exception: if its own reused desk is dirty, show a one-time, separately labeled “Setting up desk” scene; keep its provider status unchanged and do not add the scene to the activity feed.
- A cluster becomes **abandoned** only after explicit provider-reported session/thread closure with no agents remaining. An explicit agent-end signal exits that character; ordinary turn completion/failure, idle, archive, stale, or disconnected state alone does not trigger departure or haunting. An empty ended cluster keeps a neutral “Session ended” marker/time and its props, but loses its title/motif. It is softly dimmed and may show whimsical-spooky ambience: brief ghost, spider, screen-flicker-without-error-cue, or chair movement; a web persists as a prop until tidied. A returning session restores its identity/title/motif and normal lighting. A different session may reuse the physical space, but receives a new cluster identity and context rather than inheriting the old session.
- User appearance overrides are stored locally by provider identity when stable, otherwise by session. A deterministic provider-identity look is the default. Users may change sprite/outfit, palette, accessory/desk prop, and local nickname; nickname is prominent while provider name/role remain available as factual secondary identity. Do not add user-authored role/focus/task fields.

## Provider-signal interpretation

An office departure requires a genuine provider-reported agent/session end. In particular, Codex app-server `thread/closed` is runtime unload/subscription closure, not proof that the conversation ended; see [ADR 0007's protocol clarification](./0007-provider-consent-and-discovery.md#protocol-clarification-2026-10-18). It produces an unmonitored/unknown observation and must not trigger an abandoned cluster. The world model can render explicitly ended clusters when a supported provider reports them; absence of that capability is not filled with invented exits.

## Consequences

- UI tests must assert that fiction never changes normalized provider state, freshness, sorting, navigation, or the factual event feed.
- Event-driven transitions must cancel incompatible scenes immediately, without delaying a provider-reported working/approval/error state.
- Scene props and dialogue are presentation state, not session history or task artifacts; clear them when the canvas closes. Local identity preferences are separate and may persist.
- Do not reuse source-project assets or copy their code as part of adopting these patterns; review licenses separately.
